RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Clear guidance for return and exchange requests.

We want every DOMARO order to arrive in the condition you expect. Because fragrance products are personal-use items, eligibility depends on the condition of the item when it is returned.

Return and exchange windows

Under Egyptian consumer protection rules, eligible items may generally be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason, subject to applicable legal exceptions. Items received with a defect may be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery, in accordance with the law. These periods are counted from receipt of the order.

Fragrance seals and eligibility

For change-of-mind requests, fragrances must be in a condition eligible for return under applicable law. If a fragrance has been opened, sprayed, used, or its seals broken, its condition and packaging may prevent a change-of-mind return where a legal exception applies. This does not remove statutory rights for defective, damaged, or incorrect items.

Eligible requests

Unused and unopened items in their original packaging may be considered for return or exchange. Wrong items, damaged items, or products received with a manufacturing defect should be reported as soon as possible after delivery. Proof of purchase or your DOMARO order number may be required.

Opened fragrance products

For hygiene and product-integrity reasons, opened, sprayed, or used fragrances are generally not eligible for return or exchange unless the item is defective, damaged on arrival, or incorrect.

Condition of returned items

Returned items should include the original box, packaging, accessories, seals, and any included gifts where applicable. Items that show signs of use or damage after delivery may be declined.

Shipping & refunds

If DOMARO sent the wrong item or the product arrived damaged or defective, we will review the case and arrange the appropriate solution. For approved refunds, processing starts after the returned item is received and inspected. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.

How to request a return or exchange

Contact DOMARO through the Contact page and include your order number, the item concerned, and a short explanation. For damaged or incorrect items, photos may help us review the request faster.

This store policy is applied together with any mandatory consumer rights that apply to your purchase.
