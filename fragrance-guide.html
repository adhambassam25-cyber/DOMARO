How to Choose Your
Signature Fragrance

From fresh and understated to warm and unforgettable, discover the fragrance family that feels most like you.

FIND YOUR FRAGRANCE IDENTITY

A scent that becomes you.

Your signature fragrance is more than a perfume you like. It is a scent that suits your taste, the moments you wear it, and how you want to feel. Start with the notes you naturally gravitate toward, then explore how they develop on your skin.

Discover the fragrance families

01 / FRESH & CITRUS

Clean. Bright. Effortless.

Think bergamot, lemon, airy aromatics and crisp accords. A natural starting point if you enjoy light, uplifting scents for daytime and warm weather.

Best for: Everyday freshness
02 / FLORAL

Soft. Expressive. Elegant.

Rose, jasmine and other floral notes can feel delicate, modern or rich depending on the blend. Ideal if you enjoy a graceful scent with character.

Best for: A refined signature
03 / WOODY & AMBER

Warm. Confident. Deep.

Sandalwood, cedar, amber and resinous notes bring structure and warmth. Explore these families if you prefer a more grounded or enveloping scent.

Best for: Evening and cooler days
04 / MUSK & ORIENTAL

Intimate. Rich. Distinctive.

Musk can feel clean and soft, while oud, spices, tobacco and amber can add a deeper oriental character. There is plenty of variety within each profile.

Best for: A memorable presence
A SIMPLE WAY TO START

Three steps to your next favorite scent.

01 — Know your mood

Do you want something fresh for daily wear, or a deeper scent for evenings and occasions?

02 — Follow the notes

Explore the opening, heart and base notes, but remember that the whole blend matters more than one note.

03 — Give it time

Whenever possible, try a fragrance on skin and allow it to develop before deciding.
