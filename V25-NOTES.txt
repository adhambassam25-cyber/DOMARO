DOMARO V25 — CUSTOMER CENTER

New in this version:
- New CUSTOMERS tab for admins with Orders permission.
- Customer profiles are derived from existing orders and matched by normalized phone number.
- Unique customer, returning customer, total order and delivered spend KPIs.
- Customer search and filters: all, returning, one order, has delivered order.
- Customer CSV export.
- Customer profile modal with delivery areas, ordered products and full order history.
- One-click OPEN ORDER from the customer profile.
- No database migration is required for V25.

Security:
- The Customers tab is tied to the existing Orders permission.
- No new public data access is added; the UI only derives customer summaries from order data the signed-in admin is already permitted to read.
