DOMARO V26 — Advanced Business Dashboard

What's new
- Dashboard reporting period: Last 7 / 30 / 90 days / All time.
- Delivered revenue, order count, average delivered order value, delivered share.
- Revenue and order comparison against the immediately previous equivalent period.
- Unique and returning customer KPIs.
- Status breakdown for the selected period.
- Daily performance chart (latest 30 days for 90-day and all-time views).
- Top products and top governorates for the selected period.
- Recent orders scoped to the selected period.
- Dashboard CSV report export.

Important
- Run SUPABASE-V26-MIGRATION.sql before deploying the V26 frontend.
- Revenue is counted from orders whose current status is delivered.
- Trend revenue is grouped by the order creation date because DOMARO does not currently store a delivered_at timestamp.
- Top products and locations exclude cancelled orders.
- No analytics are fabricated; all figures come from live order data.
