DOMARO V19 — ENTRY COLLECTION SELECTOR

- When the homepage is first opened in a browsing session, customers see a full-screen selector.
- Choices: MEN / WOMEN / UNISEX.
- Choosing a collection sends the customer directly to the matching filtered Shop page.
- The selector appears once per browser session, so it does not interrupt normal browsing afterward.
- No Supabase or SQL changes required.
