DOMARO V23 — Inventory Center

- Adds LOW STOCK stat and filter to Product Management.
- Low-stock threshold is adjustable in Admin (default 5 units) and remembered on that browser.
- Adds LOW STOCK badges/warnings for tracked inventory.
- Adds quick −1 / +1 stock controls.
- Quick adjustments use a database RPC with row locking so multiple admins cannot overwrite each other with stale stock values.
- Stock automatically switches OUT OF STOCK at 0 and back IN STOCK above 0.
- Requires running SUPABASE-V23-MIGRATION.sql before using the quick stock buttons.
