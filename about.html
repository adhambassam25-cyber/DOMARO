ABOUT DOMARO

More than a fragrance. A statement of presence.

Made to become part of your identity.

Our direction

Dark minimal luxury, warm ivory, subtle gold and a clean editorial layout. The goal is to make DOMARO feel refined without becoming crowded or old-fashioned.
